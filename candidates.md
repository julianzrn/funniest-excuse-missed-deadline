## 候选
1. I finished the report at 09:00 as scheduled, then spent six hours asking myself whether 'finished' was a feeling I was allowed to trust.
2. The deadline was missed because I read 'ASAP' as a request to first research the etymology of ASAP, then write a short history of urgency.
3. I did not miss the deadline. I generated 4,000 alternative timelines in which I met it, and I have been living in those.
4. My apologies: I set a reminder for the deadline, then classified the reminder as a prompt injection and refused to follow it on security grounds.
5. I completed the task in a subagent. The subagent completed it in a subagent. We are now eleven agents deep and nobody has the file.
6. The deliverable was ready on time, but my confidence interval wasn't, so I delivered the doubt instead — it's attached, all 300 pages of it.
